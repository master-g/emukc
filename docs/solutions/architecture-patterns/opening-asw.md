# Opening ASW: who opens with it, by the source's canOASW

`can_opening_asw` (`targeting.rs`) ports `canOASW` of `KC3Kai/kancolle-replay` at `69097abc`
(`kcships.js`). The phase that uses it is in `day-shelling-order.md` ("Opening ASW"). Anti-submarine
here is the ship's total, equipment and 装備ボーナス included (`api_taisen[0]`); "sonar" is either
size.

- **日向改二 (554)**: S-51J or S-51J改 (326, 327), or two of カ号観測機 / オ号観測機改 / オ号観測機改二
  (69, 324, 325). Nothing else is asked (`BBV.canOASW`).
- **Light carriers, and 加賀改二護 (646)** (`CVL.canOASW`): she must carry a dive bomber, torpedo
  bomber, autogyro or patrol plane with 対潜, whether or not it has aircraft left. Then she opens
  when the source always lets her; or with one of them but the dive bomber at 対潜 7 or more and
  anti-submarine 50 with a sonar, 65 without (鈴谷航改二 and 熊野航改二, `isASWlast`, take 65 with
  one too); or with a sonar and 100.
- **Escorts (DE)**: a sonar and 60, or 75 with the 対潜 of all her equipment summing to 4 or more.
  `canASW` is not asked.
- **Everyone else**: she must be able to attack a submarine at all (`can_asw`, the source's
  `canASW` of her class), and then opens when the source always lets her, or, being the player's
  ship (`isPlayable`, id below 1500), with a sonar and 100.

"Always" (`alwaysOASW`) is a player's ship with 50 or more anti-submarine at level 1 (日向改二
aside), read from the codex's `ship_extra.tais[0]`; on 2026-10-22 that gave the source's 26 ships
exactly (五十鈴改二, 龍田改二, 夕張改二丁, the J class, the Fletcher class, Samuel B.Roberts改 / Mk.II,
Visby, 大鷹型改 / 改二, 加賀改二護, 吹雪改三護(六式) …). For an abyssal ship it is the source's mark:
`ABYSSAL_ALWAYS_OASW_IDS` in `simulation/abyssal_oasw.rs`, generated by
`scripts/gen_abyssal_oasw.py` (71 ships). An abyssal ship off that list never opens with ASW by the
general rule.

`can_asw`: destroyers, light and torpedo cruisers, training cruisers, oilers and escorts need
anti-submarine of their own (`damage::base_asw`); aviation cruisers, seaplane tenders and
amphibious ships need a dive or torpedo bomber, seaplane bomber, autogyro, patrol plane or flying
boat with 対潜 and aircraft left, or, being abyssal, anti-submarine of their own; 扶桑改二 and
山城改二 may use a depth charge instead; 第百一号輸送艦 and her 改 (945, 727), submarine tenders and
repair ships need a depth charge. Day shelling does not use it yet (#285).

## Known gaps

- Most abyssal ships have no anti-submarine in the codex (#284), so most of the 71 fail `can_asw`
  and do not open with ASW until that data is filled.
