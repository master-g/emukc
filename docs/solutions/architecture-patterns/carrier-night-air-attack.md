# The carrier night air attack

## What

A carrier attacks at night in one of two ways, and otherwise not at all
(`targeting.rs` `can_attack_night_ship`):

- **By air** (`simulation/night.rs` `can_night_air_attack`). She needs a night air group —
  夜間作戦航空要員 or a built-in 夜戦特性 (`EXEMPT_NIGHT_CV_IDS`), and a 夜間戦闘機, 夜間攻撃機
  or 夜間爆戦 with aircraft left in its slot — or to be Ark Royal with Swordfish. And a deck
  that works: more than half her hull, more than a quarter for an armoured carrier.
- **By gunfire** (`NIGHT_SHELLING_CV_IDS`): Graf Zeppelin, Saratoga, 大鷹改二 and the like
  shell as a surface ship does until heavily damaged, with the ordinary night formula. The
  abyssal carriers the source marks `nightattack` attack the same way (see "Enemy carriers").

しまね丸改 (1008), a 補給艦, flies by air under the same rules as a carrier (the source's
`canAirAttackNB`, `AO.prototype.loadEquips`): a night air group, more than half her hull, the
carrier cut-ins and the carrier's power (`flies_at_night_as_carrier`). Without it, or below half
her hull, she shells as any ship does. しまね丸 and the other oilers do not fly.

光電管彗星, 岩井爆戦 and Swordfish fly with a night air group and count in its power and its
cut-ins, but do not make one. So 要員 with ordinary bombers is not enough: such a carrier
stays out of the night, where she used to attack.

## Where the rules come from

`KC3Kai/kancolle-replay` at `69097abc`: `kcships.js` `hasNBAirGear`, `canNBAirAttack`,
`CV.canNB`, `CV.NBPower`; `kcsim.js` 869-903 for the proficiency and the packet flag;
`kcSHIPDATA.js` for `hasBuiltInNightCrew` and `nightattack`. The client's own
`_getNormalAttackType` (night) keeps the list of carriers it draws as gunfire, which is the
source's `nightattack` plus Wasp and Wasp改. The source predates those two; that they shell at
night is from the wiki's Wasp page (「火力初期値が1以上のため夜間砲撃を行う。改も同様」). All eleven
are in `NIGHT_SHELLING_CV_IDS`. The wiki's reason is not a rule to derive the list from: nine other
carriers in the master data start with firepower above zero. Six of them (Langley, Independence,
Gambier Bay Mk.II and their remodels) the client does not draw as gunfire, so the list stays a
list.

The other three are Béarn's forms, and they are in the list by inference: right after the
eleven the client draws Béarn (1055) and Béarn改 (1060) as gunfire, and Béarn amélioration
(1061) as gunfire or, with a torpedo, a torpedo attack, and all three start with firepower
above zero. Nothing read says outright that they attack at night (the wiki's Béarn page does
not mention night battle). With a torpedo 1061 still attacks with the ordinary night formula;
the client picks the animation from her equipment.

## Enemy carriers

`simulation/abyssal_night.rs` holds two tables, generated by
`scripts/gen_abyssal_night_carriers.py` from the source's `kcSHIPDATA.js`: the 132 abyssal
carriers with a `nightattack` mark, and the 74 with `hasBuiltInNightCrew`.

- Mark 1 (86) and mark 2 (11: 離島棲姫, リコリス棲姫, 深海海月姫) attack until heavily damaged.
  The two differ only in what is drawn: the client draws リコリス棲姫 and 深海海月姫 as gunfire
  by name, and every other carrier as aircraft, whatever `api_n_mother_list` says.
- Mark 3 (35 light carriers) attacks a surface ship only while the fleet she attacks has a
  searchlight lit: the player's, when she is the enemy (`nightPhaseTarget`'s `light` is the
  target fleet's). A fleet's searchlight is lit for the whole night if a ship afloat carries
  one; it is read once, when the night begins. Its other effects are not modelled.
- A carrier with a crew of her own flies her night attack by the rules above: the same hull
  limits, the same power, flag 1. She needs the aircraft type only, not aircraft left in the
  slot (the source does not ask an enemy). 夜深海艦爆, 夜深海艦爆II and 深海不屈艦爆 Mk.II count
  in the power as 光電管彗星 does. 22 light carriers have a crew and no mark: they attack only
  while they can fly.
- A carrier with a crew of her own rolls the carrier cut-ins as the player's do (`CV.NBtypes`
  has no side check): her crew stands in for the 要員, and her aircraft count by type whether or
  not any are left in the slot. 夜猫深海艦戦 and the like are night fighters (icon 45), 夜復讐深海艦攻
  and the like night attackers (46); 夜深海艦爆 and the like count as Swordfish do, towards 64.
  The marked carriers without a crew shell, and make none.
- Neither the source nor the codex gives an enemy's aircraft any proficiency (the source loads
  enemy equipment with ranks of 0, the codex with `api_alv` none), and the 装備ボーナス table has
  no abyssal equipment, so her torpedo fit bonus is 0: there is nothing more to port there.
- The enemy fires by position, as the player does. The source's "enemy always skips to the
  next capable ship" is its NPC friend fleet's night only.
- An enemy light carrier attacks a submarine at night by the rule above: anti-submarine of
  her own above zero. `bare_asw` reads it from the codex's enemy data, which takes the source's
  `ASW` for every abyssal ship (`abyssal_stats.json`, #284): 40 light carriers (30 marked 3, 10
  with a crew and no mark) have some. One marked 3 takes a submarine without the searchlight; a
  submarine is all she can take in the dark. After a day battle such a hit is a scratch, as any;
  in a night-start battle its power counts that figure as her own anti-submarine
  (`damage::base_asw`, the source's `statsBase.ASW`).

## The power

`night_air_power` replaces `火力 + 雷装 + 5 + 改修` for an attack by air; the night scout,
the cut-in's multiplier, the cap and the ammunition follow as for any night attack.

- The hull's own firepower and torpedo: master data plus 近代化改修 (`api_kyouka`). Not
  `api_karyoku`, which also holds the equipment and its fit bonuses.
- For each night aircraft: its firepower, `sqrt(改修)`, its torpedo if it is a 艦攻 and its
  bombing if it is not, 3 for each aircraft in the slot, and
  `0.45 x (火力 + 雷装 + 対潜 + 爆装) x sqrt(aircraft)`.
- 光電管彗星, 岩井爆戦 and Swordfish: no count term, and 0.3 for 0.45.
- Ark Royal without a night air group: her Swordfish' firepower, torpedo and `sqrt(改修)`.
- The fit bonus on her torpedo (`carrier_torpedo_bonus`, the source's `getEquipBonusCVTorp` with
  no slot): one plane's share of her 装備ボーナス torpedo, and the smallest share among her aviation
  personnel. Each piece's share is what the bonus gains when it goes on after the pieces before it
  (`loadEquips`). The plane is 522 and 523 together if she has either, else the first she carries
  of `TORPEDO_BONUS_FIRST`, then of the other planes with a share, by id downwards.
- Against a land target neither that bonus nor a 艦攻's torpedo counts; the hull's torpedo, the
  Swordfish' on Ark Royal and the share term stay, as in the source.

The source's special-aircraft multipliers (`getBonusSpecialPlane`, `bonusSpecialP`) are event
bonuses its user enters for a map; nothing in its data sets them, so they are 1 here.

## Proficiency

An attack by air takes the ship's `plane_proficiency`, the same three numbers the day shelling
of a carrier takes: accuracy and critical rate in `roll_attack`, critical damage in
`calculate_night_damage`. One draw an attack, as everywhere.

The day attack on a submarine takes it too (`asw_proficiency`), for the ships whose aircraft
make it — 航巡, 航戦, 水母, 揚陸艦, 軽空母 — and not in the opening (`Aim::in_the_opening`).

## Whom she attacks

A carrier flying her night air attack goes for the surface ships and takes a submarine only once
none is left (`nightPhaseTarget`; `AttackCapability::BothPreferSurface`). A carrier that shells
takes the submarine first, as any ship does.

Which carriers may attack a submarine at night at all is `canASWNight`: a light carrier whose own
anti-submarine is above zero, aircraft or not (`bare_asw`: grown with her level plus 近代化改修,
without equipment or what it grants); a fleet or armoured carrier never. The attack is not flown.

## A submarine at night

Any ship's night attack on a submarine is the day one (`ASW`, called from the night loop with
`isNightDmg` and `isNightActual`), never a cut-in or a double attack:

- The roll is the day roll on a submarine, `Aim::new(AttackKind::Asw, ..)`: base 80, twice the
  anti-submarine of each sonar, the formation's anti-submarine column, morale, and the
  proficiency of `asw_proficiency`. Not the night gunfire roll, so it can miss.
- After a day battle the attack power counts as 0: a hit is scratch damage
  (`calculate_scratch_damage`) and the submarine cannot be sunk. Not for a combined fleet's
  escort, on either side (`is_escort_deck`): the source hands `ASW` `!NBonly && !isescort`, so its
  attack does real damage as in a night-start battle.
- In a night-start battle (開幕夜戦, `NightBattleInput::night_start`, set by
  `execute_sp_midnight` alone) the damage is `calculate_asw_damage` as by day, with formation,
  engagement and the attacker's damage state, and she can be sunk.

The packet does not change: `api_sp_list` 0, `api_n_mother_list` 0, the depth charge in
`api_si_list`.

Two guns between main and secondary make a double attack (`NBtypes`), so Graf Zeppelin with two
secondary guns makes one. That is her gunfire only: a carrier flying with a night air group has
the carrier cut-ins and no other attack type (`CV.NBtypes`), so with the same two guns and a
night air group she flies a plain night air attack, flag 1.

## The carrier cut-ins

`carrier_night_cut_ins` lists every one of the source's four (`NBATTACKDATA` 61 to 64,
`CV.NBtypes`) that her air group makes, in this order, and `resolve_night_attack` rolls them in
turn as it rolls a destroyer's: one roll each until one comes up, a plain flown attack if none
does. The counts are of slots with aircraft left: night fighters F, night attackers A, night
bombers B (icons 45, 46, 58), 光電管彗星, and Swordfish and 岩井.

| | Makes it | Damage | Accuracy | Chance divided by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 61 | F ≥ 2, A ≥ 1 | 1.25 | 1.25 | 1.05 |
| 62 | F ≥ 1, A ≥ 1 | 1.2 | 1.2 | 1.2 |
| 63 | 光電管彗星 or B, and F + A + B + 光電管彗星 ≥ 2 | 1.2 | 1.2 | 1.2 |
| 64 | F ≥ 1, and F + A + B + 光電管彗星 + Swordfish + 岩井 ≥ 3 | 1.18 | 1.2 | 1.3 |

So two night fighters and a night attacker roll 61, then 62, then 64. One night fighter with one
Swordfish makes none; wikiwiki's 戦他他 had been read as two aircraft before the source was
followed. All four go out as `api_sp_list` 6 and name the same aircraft in `api_si_list`.

## What the client is told

`api_n_mother_list` is 1 for an attack by air. The client keeps it as `yasen_kubo` and, when
set, draws the attack as aircraft (`PhaseAttackKansaiki`) whatever the ship is. The cut-in
carries it as well. It is 0 for a double attack and for an attack on a submarine, as in the
source.

`night_turn` decides once whether the attack is flown and hands that on: the power goes to
`calculate_night_damage` as `air_power`, the roll gets `Aim::as_night_air_attack`. `accuracy.rs`
and `damage.rs` do not ask `simulation::night` themselves.

## How it is checked

- `night.rs` tests: who flies (crew, aircraft, empty slot, Ark Royal, the two hull limits),
  who shells, one power worked by hand, the proficiency on the damage and on the draw, the flag.
- The `night_air_attack` preset (six 赤城改二戊 with night fighters; the first three with a
  光電管彗星, a night attacker and two Swordfish, for 63, 62 and 64) is in the sim→validate gate with `PhaseExpectation::NightAirAttack`: the night gate
  asserts that this preset flies and that no other does.
- `make headless-check SCENARIO=night_air_attack` fights 2-1 with it in the real client and
  wants each cut-in to show the aircraft of her place; 63, 62 and 64 have played. The first
  three are short of ammunition, so the battle reaches the night (28 seeds in 30).
- `abyssal_carrier_tests` in `night.rs`: the tables against what the script prints, who
  attacks, the searchlight, one power worked by hand, Béarn. The `enemy_night_carrier` preset
  (2-5, cell C) is in the gate with `PhaseExpectation::EnemyCarrierNightAttack`, and in the
  headless check. A crewed enemy (flag 1) is met only in 5-6's fourth phase and in 7-4; the headless
  scenario `enemy_night_crew` meets 7-4's ヒ船団棲姫 at M and the client flies her attack, and
  her 62 when it comes up; `enemy_night_crew_5_6` meets 5-6's 南太平洋空母棲姫 at X in the fourth
  phase, whose flown attack the client plays as aircraft (three runs, 2026-10-15).
- Mark 2's gunfire cannot be played in a regular map: its only ones there (離島棲姫 1668 and 1669)
  are at 6-4 D, F, G and I, all air-raid cells (`event_kind` 6) with no night, and 6-4's boss
  (1671, 1672) has no mark. It waits for an event map.

## Known gaps

- Bombing bonuses (`DBCrew`): the 装備ボーナス table carries no bombing. The torpedo fit bonus
  itself is taken by day too (#260): her day shelling adds the whole bonus to her torpedo, gone
  with it on land (`carrier_gun_power`); her airstrike adds the plane bonus to one slot, the one
  with the highest torpedo (a dive bomber's bombing), then the most aircraft, and the crew bonus
  to each 艦攻, on land too (`carrier_slot_torpedo_bonus`, `getEquipBonusCVTorp(slot)`).
- The 夜間瑞雲 cut-in is in `night-attack-types.md`; smoke in `smoke-screen.md`.
- The types of the other ships are in `night-attack-types.md`.
