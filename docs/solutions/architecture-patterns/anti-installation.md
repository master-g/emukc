# Land targets: a generated table of kinds, the source's multipliers ported by hand

## What a land target is

`target_class` asks `installation_type`: an abyssal ship with an `installtype` in the source's
`kcSHIPDATA.js` (`KC3Kai/kancolle-replay` at `69097abc`) is an installation, of one of six kinds:
1 soft-skinned, 2 pillbox, 3 supply depot, 4 isolated island, 5 northern, 6 harbour summer princess.
The table is `simulation/installation_types.rs`, generated by `scripts/gen_installation_types.py`;
do not edit it by hand. The test `the_installation_tables_follow_the_source` pins the counts the
script prints (66, 16, 62, 11, 3, 16; the source's six further soft-skinned entries from id 3001 are
its own Vita ships and are left out).

The same script lists five marks that are not land targets but take their own multipliers:
`isSupplyDepot` (the vacation-mode 集積地棲姫III), `isAnchorage` (vacation-mode 泊地水鬼), `isDock`
(船渠棲姫), `isSummerBB` (戦艦夏姫), `isSummerCA` (重巡夏姫).

Before the table, installations were found by name markers (砲台, 飛行場, 港湾, 離島, 集積地, 泊地,
要塞, トーチカ). Switching changed 61 ships:

- Now installations: 北方棲姫, 北方棲妹, 中間棲姫, リコリス棲姫, 中枢棲姫, 北端上陸姫, 対空小鬼,
  深海上陸小鬼 (33 ids).
- Now ships at sea: 浮遊要塞, 護衛要塞, 泊地棲鬼, 泊地棲姫, 環礁空母泊地棲姫, and the vacation-mode
  泊地水鬼 and 集積地棲姫III (28 ids).

Every abyssal ship in the codex is in the source, so the name markers are kept only for a ship newer
than the source's last abyssal id (`LAST_SOURCE_ABYSSAL_ID`), with a warning to regenerate.

## What equipment does against them

`AntiInstallation::of` (`installation.rs`) ports `Ship.prototype.loadEquips` with the source's
`installRevamp` and `panzerIIIBuff` on, as they are by default. It counts the pieces the source
counts (`installeqs`): the landing craft by class (type 24 the first, 166/449/514 the second, the
特型内火艇 of type 46 the third) and their stars, the shells, seaplane and dive bombers, WG42, the
mortars and rockets, each named tank and the army units. From them:

- `installModAll` and `installFlat`: each of the tanks and army units multiplies both and then adds
  to the flat power, in the source's order; an AB boat or an armed daihatsu with the pieces they
  pair with (`abSynergyMult`) does the same; WG42, the mortars and the rockets add flat power by
  how many.
- A multiplier per kind of target, `installModAll` folded into all but the northern one:
  `softSkinMult` for a soft-skinned target or a supply depot, `pillboxMult` (1.4 to start for a
  destroyer or light cruiser), `isoMult`, `northernMult`, `harbourSummerMult`. The source multiplies
  the harbour's by its rockets' 1.25 and 1.4 whatever their number; it is kept so.
- Their day parts, `softSkinMultDay` and the like (1.1, 1.3, 1.3 and 1.5 with an AB boat or armed
  daihatsu, a second step with two of them or two 特四式内火艇; the source leaves the part unset
  without the first, so the second does nothing alone). The northern one has none.
- The multipliers after the cap (`post_cap`): a supply depot's (`installtype` 3 or
  `isSupplyDepot`), the anchorage's, the dock's and the summer battleship's and heavy cruiser's,
  the last three with the source's lists of ship ids and classes that take more. The source's
  switches for the dock and the summer ships are on by default, and so they are here.

Where it is read (`damage.rs`):

- By day (`day_gun_power`, the source's `shellPower`): firepower, 30 more for a submarine, 5, the
  combined fleet's correction and the improvement bonus, times the multiplier and its day part,
  plus the flat power. The light cruiser's gun fit is added after, as the source adds `FPfit`.
- At night (`night_gun_power`, `NBPower`): firepower, 30 more for a submarine and the improvement
  bonus, times the multiplier, plus the flat power. No torpedo, but for the northern princesses.
- At night, a ship with nothing equipped at all: firepower and torpedo, no multiplier and no
  submarine's 30. This is wikiwiki's rule (「戦闘について」 夜戦: 「対陸上でも、素手…の場合に限り雷装値が
  有効になる」), which the source does not have; the user chose it on 2026-10-15. Day shelling
  counts no torpedo against any target, so the rule has nothing to change there.
- After the cap and any cut-in's multiplier, before a critical (`post_cap_multiplier`): by day
  and at night all five; by torpedo the dock's and the summer ships' (the source's
  `anchorageTorpNerf` leaves the anchorage out, and a supply depot is not torpedoed).

## Known gaps

- The airstrike's own bonuses against land targets and special ships (`enableAirstrikeSpecialBonus`,
  with a roll of its own) and its dock and summer multipliers.
- The summer carrier, French battleship and European princess multipliers (`isSummerCV`,
  `isFrenchBB`, `isEuroWP`, 19 ships in the source, switches on by default): plan 2026-10-15-004 did
  not take them, and the generator does not list them.
- A carrier's day shelling of a land target (`CV.prototype.shellPower`) keeps the carrier formula
  without the multipliers.
- A carrier with dive bombers that cannot attack land does not shell a land target in the source
  (`CV.canShellInstallation`, `divebomberInstall`); here she still does. In #136's body, not in
  plan 2026-10-15-004.
- The source never lets かばう touch a land target (`choiceWProtect` returns a targeted
  installation, and an installation does not shield its flagship); `select_escort_shield` does not
  know this.
