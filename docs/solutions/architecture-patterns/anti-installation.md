# Land targets: a generated table of kinds, the source's multipliers ported by hand

## What a land target is

`target_class` asks `installation_type`: an abyssal ship with an `installtype` in the source's
`kcSHIPDATA.js` (`KC3Kai/kancolle-replay` at `69097abc`) is an installation, of one of six kinds:
1 soft-skinned, 2 pillbox, 3 supply depot, 4 isolated island, 5 northern, 6 harbour summer princess.
The table is `simulation/installation_types.rs`, generated by `scripts/gen_installation_types.py`;
do not edit it by hand. The test `the_installation_tables_follow_the_source` pins the counts the
script prints (66, 16, 62, 11, 3, 16; the source's six further soft-skinned entries from id 3001 are
its own Vita ships and are left out).

The same script lists five marks that are not land targets but take their own multipliers:
`isSupplyDepot` (the vacation-mode 集積地棲姫III), `isAnchorage` (vacation-mode 泊地水鬼), `isDock`
(船渠棲姫), `isSummerBB` (戦艦夏姫), `isSummerCA` (重巡夏姫).

Before the table, installations were found by name markers (砲台, 飛行場, 港湾, 離島, 集積地, 泊地,
要塞, トーチカ). Switching changed 61 ships:

- Now installations: 北方棲姫, 北方棲妹, 中間棲姫, リコリス棲姫, 中枢棲姫, 北端上陸姫, 対空小鬼,
  深海上陸小鬼 (33 ids).
- Now ships at sea: 浮遊要塞, 護衛要塞, 泊地棲鬼, 泊地棲姫, 環礁空母泊地棲姫, and the vacation-mode
  泊地水鬼 and 集積地棲姫III (28 ids).

Every abyssal ship in the codex is in the source, so the name markers are kept only for a ship newer
than the source's last abyssal id (`LAST_SOURCE_ABYSSAL_ID`), with a warning to regenerate.

## Known gaps

- The anti-installation equipment's multipliers and flat bonuses are modelled neither by day nor at
  night.
- The source never lets かばう touch a land target (`choiceWProtect` returns a targeted
  installation, and an installation does not shield its flagship); `select_escort_shield` does not
  know this.
