# Opening ASW: who opens with it, by the source's canOASW

`can_opening_asw` (`targeting.rs`) ports `canOASW` of `KC3Kai/kancolle-replay` at `69097abc`
(`kcships.js`). The phase that uses it is in `day-shelling-order.md` ("Opening ASW"). Anti-submarine
here is the ship's total, equipment and 装備ボーナス included (`api_taisen[0]`); "sonar" is either
size.

- **日向改二 (554)**: S-51J or S-51J改 (326, 327), or two of カ号観測機 / オ号観測機改 / オ号観測機改二
  (69, 324, 325). Nothing else is asked here (`BBV.canOASW`).
- **Light carriers, and 加賀改二護 (646)** (`CVL.canOASW`): she must carry a dive bomber, torpedo
  bomber, autogyro or patrol plane with 対潜, whether or not it has aircraft left (her turn asks
  that, below). Then she opens when the source always lets her; or with one of them but the dive
  bomber at 対潜 7 or more and anti-submarine 50 with a sonar, 65 without (鈴谷航改二 and
  熊野航改二, `isASWlast`, take 65 with one too); or with a sonar and 100.
- **Escorts (DE)**: a sonar and 60, or 75 with the 対潜 of all her equipment summing to 4 or more.
  `canASW` is not asked here.
- **Everyone else**: she must be able to attack a submarine at all (`can_asw`, the source's
  `canASW` of her class), and then opens when the source always lets her, or, being the player's
  ship (`isPlayable`, id below 1500), with a sonar and 100. Carriers other than 646, armoured ones
  included, never do: the source's CV has no `canASW`.

When her turn comes (`has_opening_asw_shot`, the source's `shellPhaseTarget`, `kcsim.js:1086`)
every ship must still be able to attack a submarine (`can_asw`), which 日向改二, the light
carriers and the escorts meet only here: one whose planes of that kind the air battle took keeps
her place in the order and does not fire.
鈴谷航改二 and 熊野航改二 hold while a surface ship is afloat.

"Always" (`alwaysOASW`) is a ship the source marks (`simulation/always_oasw.rs`, generated by
`scripts/gen_always_oasw.py`: 24 of the player's, 71 abyssal), or a player's ship with 50 or more
anti-submarine at level 1 (日向改二 aside), read from the codex's `ship_extra.tais[0]`. Together
they are the source's 26 (五十鈴改二, 龍田改二, 夕張改二丁, the J class, the Fletcher class, Samuel
B.Roberts改 / Mk.II, Visby / 改, 大鷹型改 / 改二, 加賀改二護, 吹雪改三護(六式) …); the test
`the_player_ships_always_opening_with_asw_are_the_sources` fails if the codex's level-1 figures
drift from the source's `ASWbase`. An abyssal ship off the list never opens with ASW by the
general rule.

`can_asw`: destroyers, light and torpedo cruisers, training cruisers, oilers and escorts need
anti-submarine of their own (`damage::base_asw`); aviation cruisers, seaplane tenders and
amphibious ships need a dive or torpedo bomber, seaplane bomber, autogyro, patrol plane or flying
boat with 対潜 and aircraft left, or, being abyssal, anti-submarine of their own; light carriers
and 646 need one of those with aircraft left, or, being abyssal, nothing; 扶桑改二 and
山城改二 may use a depth charge instead; 第百一号輸送艦 and her 改 (945, 727), submarine tenders and
repair ships need a depth charge. Day shelling does not use it yet (#285).
